SEKHAR ASSOCIATES • ABOUT US

A Legacy of Integrity & Justice

Delivering unwavering legal counsel and formidable courtroom defense across Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana for more than two decades.

Standing Firm With Our Clients

Founded by Mr. S. Chandra Sekhar (LL.M. Osmania University, LL.B., 20+ years of active courtroom experience), Sekhar Associates Law Firm has evolved into a premier full-service practice taking up cases from all over Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Based in Kurnool with active appearances before District Courts and the High Courts at Amaravati and Hyderabad, our chambers combine seasoned trial wisdom with modern jurisprudence.

Our senior partners, including Advocate B. Nagalakshmi Devi (LL.M., LL.B. Prasuna Law College, 15+ years experience, speaking English, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada) and S. Deekshitha (B.A., LL.B. Hons., Prasuna Law College), lead a dedicated bench of more than 10+ specialized legal professionals. With over 3000+ cases & clients successfully represented, our chambers are equipped to solve all kinds of cases with meticulous attention, transparency, and fearless advocacy.

TWO DECADES OF DEDICATED ADVOCACY

Chambers Milestones

Evolution of Sekhar Associates Law Firm from 2004 to Present

2004

Foundation of Chambers in Kurnool

Sri S. Chandra Sekhar established the practice following enrollment with the Bar Council, initiating trial advocacy and civil dispute representation before District Courts.

2009

Family & Matrimonial Division Expansion

Advocate B. Nagalakshmi Devi joined the chambers as Senior Partner, establishing our comprehensive matrimonial, domestic relations, and title scrutiny divisions.

2018

Commercial Arbitration & IBC Wings

Inaugurated dedicated practice units handling commercial arbitrations, Section 9 interim measures, Section 11 arbitrator appointments, and NCLT insolvency proceedings.

2022

Young Advocate Induction & Digital Chambers

Induction of new generation advocates including S. Deekshitha (B.A., LL.B. Hons, Prasuna Law College) and expanding our research bench to more than 10+ specialized legal professionals across AP & Telangana.

2026

Multi-Disciplinary Chambers Network

A full-service legal institution representing clients across District Courts, Special Commercial Tribunals, and the Hon’ble High Court of Andhra Pradesh & Telangana.
