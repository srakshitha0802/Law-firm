Welcome to Sekhar Associates Law Firm

Founded and led by Mr. S. Chandra Sekhar (LL.M. Osmania University, LL.B., 20+ years of active courtroom experience), Sekhar Associates is a premier full-service law firm based in Kurnool. We regularly accept and represent cases from all over Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, appearing before District Courts, Sessions Courts, Special Appellate Tribunals, and the High Courts at Amaravati and Hyderabad.

With senior leadership comprising B. Nagalakshmi Devi (LL.M., LL.B. Prasuna Law College, 15+ years experience, fluent in English, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada) and S. Deekshitha (B.A., LL.B. Hons., Prasuna Law College), backed by more than 10+ specialized legal professionals, our chambers possess the experience and trial-readiness to solve all kinds of cases with meticulous attention and unwavering client loyalty.
